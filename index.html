About

I design, build, and teach real-time embedded systems — from bare-metal firmware on ARM7 and 8051 cores to communication stacks that get sensors, controllers, and displays talking to each other reliably. Today I work as an Embedded Systems Trainer, taking students and working engineers from "what's a microcontroller" to shipping firmware that survives contact with real hardware.
